Business Operations Handbook

The company operates in multiple regions and follows a customer-centric approach. Employees are expected to maintain professionalism and provide high-quality service to customers.

Working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid leave annually.

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year. Employees who exceed expectations may receive bonuses and promotions.

The company encourages continuous learning and provides access to professional development courses.

Remote work is permitted for eligible employees with manager approval.
